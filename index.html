MISSION OF CBS

The mission of the Character Building Society (CBS) is to develop responsible, ethical, and service-oriented individuals by promoting character, leadership, integrity, discipline, compassion, and social responsibility among students.

CBS aims to provide students with opportunities to:

  • Develop strong moral and ethical values.
  • Promote Khidmat-e-Khalq (service to humanity) and a spirit of compassion.
  • Encourage Amr bil Ma'ruf wa Nahi 'anil Munkar through positive and respectful means.
  • Develop leadership, communication, teamwork, and decision-making skills.
  • Support students in achieving their academic and personal goals.
  • Encourage active participation in community service and constructive university activities.
  • Build a culture of mutual respect, responsibility, discipline, and unity.
  • Prepare students to become responsible citizens and contribute positively to society and the nation.

Our mission is to build not only successful students, but individuals of strong character who lead with integrity, serve humanity, and create a positive impact wherever they go.
